Moves
Coach tips:
1. Captures are mandatory — scan for jumps before anything else.
2. Move toward the center, not the edges.
3. Advance your pieces — kings win games.
4. Trade pawns to simplify when you are ahead.
5. Guard your back row — don't let him king easily.
6. Fork: one capture that sets up another (multi-jump).
