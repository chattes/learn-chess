Coach tips:
1. Never draw the third side of a box — opponent takes it free.
2. Completing a box scores AND gives you another turn — chains win games.
3. Early game: spread lines, don't open boxes for him.
4. Late game: count remaining lines; hand him a chain only if you must.
5. When one 2-line chain is left, force him to open it for you.

How to Play Dots and Boxes

Goal: claim more boxes than the bot. You play first.

Basics

  • The board shows a grid of dots with boxes between them.
  • On your turn, draw a single line between two adjacent dots (click or tap the space between dots).
  • If your line completes the 4th side of a box, you claim that box and draw again.
  • Chain several boxes in one turn by completing them one after another.

End of game

  • Game ends when every box is claimed.
  • Most boxes wins.

Beginner tips

  • Third side of any box is poison — never draw it unless forced.
  • Look ahead: after your move, could the bot take a box and then another in the same chain?
  • Late game, count the chains — sometimes giving one small box forces him to open a bigger one.
