How to Play Othello (Reversi)

Goal: have the most discs of your color on the board when it is full. You play Black and move first.

Basics

  • Board starts with 4 discs in the center: two black, two white, diagonal.
  • On your turn, place one disc on an empty square.
  • Your new disc must sandwich one or more enemy discs in a straight line (row, column or diagonal) between itself and another of your discs.
  • All sandwiched enemy discs flip to your color.
  • If no square sandwiches anything, you pass (the bot moves).

End of game

  • Game ends when both players pass in a row (usually the board is full or no legal moves remain).
  • Count your discs — most discs wins.

Beginner tips

  • Grab corners first — once taken they never flip.
  • Give up early discs for corner and edge positions.
  • Avoid the X and C squares (next to empty corners) — they hand him the corner.
  • Keep your options open: pieces with many moves beat pieces hoarded.
